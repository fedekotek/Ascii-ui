Rules

    

    
@@ %% ## ** ++ == :: ..

    
    
      
  • Weight is character density. @ is the loudest thing on the page and . is the quietest.
    • 
      
  • Frames, rules, fills and shadows are strings of characters. Hierarchy comes from which character.
    • 
      
  • Big type is a bitmap: every pixel of a headline is a character.
    • 
      
  • Blue means focus. Nothing else is blue.
    • 
      
  • Nothing jumps. State changes travel through the ramp, one character at a time, at a low frame rate.
    • 
      
  • Underneath it is plain HTML: buttons, inputs, a dialog.
